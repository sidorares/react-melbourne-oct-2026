## Tests and specs help a lot. They can't replace eyeballing

<Grid cols={3} gap={32}>

<Card variant="top" tone="orange" title="Green suite, 700× slower">

A shadow blur re-run on every composite. Requests, bytes, composites, area and the rendered pixels were all identical before and after the fix.

</Card>

<Card variant="top" tone="blue" title="Obvious once somebody looked">

Text 3 px low, a badge riding high, a header that never lined up with its table. Each one passed.

</Card>

<Card variant="top" title="So: gut feeling, and demos">

Hover it, drag it, resize it. The stress demo exists to be looked at.

</Card>

</Grid>

<Notes>

The blur bug was found by hovering a card and feeling the lag. No metric moved until a new one was added that counts convolved pixels. Every visual defect in the examples rework passed a green suite and was obvious the moment somebody looked.

</Notes>
